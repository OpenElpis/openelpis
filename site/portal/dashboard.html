Why not just use ChatGPT or Gemini?

General chatbots are built to sound convincing — and they will confidently invent studies, numbers and citations that don't exist. OpenElpis is the opposite: it answers only from validated, expert-reviewed articles, and every claim shows a real source you can open. If the evidence isn't there, it says so — it never guesses.

It puts the evidence in front of you, so you stay in control.
